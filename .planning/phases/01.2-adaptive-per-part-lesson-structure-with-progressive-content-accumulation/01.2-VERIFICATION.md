### Human Verification Required

#### 1. Visual rendering of grouped content

**Test:** Run `node learn/bin/gsd-learn.cjs` and navigate to a code-heavy lesson (e.g., Lesson 2 in either module).

**Expected:**
- Part 1: Code block and its following explanation text appear together as one group
- One focus line per group (not separate lines for code and text within group)
- One bridge section per group after all items in the group
- Progress dots show "Part X of Y" where Y is group count (e.g., "Part 1 of 7" for a lesson with 11 items)
- Horizontal rules between groups, NOT between code and text within a group
- 'q' to go back removes entire group
- Standalone text blocks appear as their own individual parts

**Why human:** ANSI styling and layout coherence are visual properties that cannot be asserted programmatically in a non-TTY environment.

#### 2. Focus and bridge content quality review (GEN-02 quality)

**Test:** Read focus lines and bridge sections while navigating lessons.

**Expected:**
- Focus phrases are 3-8 words describing what the group teaches
- Bridge sentences create narrative flow between groups
- Last group in each lesson references the next lesson title
- Lesson 6 final bridge references module completion

**Why human:** Content quality is subjective. Structural validation passes but pedagogical effectiveness requires human judgment.
